How they playpressure, the box, what offenses try, discipline

Pressure and the blitz · FTN charting and PFR

The most aggressive defense in football. They send extra rushers on 71% of dropbacks and average 4.9 rushers; only 29% of dropbacks see four or fewer. It works both ways: -0.20 EPA allowed when they blitz, -0.26 when they do not.
