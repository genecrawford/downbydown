Vikings offense vs Lions defense

Week 9 at Ford Field, Sunday 1:00 PM. Season numbers through Week 8: six games for each side.

Biggest edge

Tight ends feast

Detroit gives up 100 yards a game to tight ends, the most in the league and nearly double the 54 average. Minnesota's tight end targets rank 3rd in EPA. Good news for Hockenson.

Opportunity

Shots downfield

The Lions are 32nd in explosive plays allowed and bleed deep middle and deep right. Minnesota's best zone is deep left, but only 20% of their throws go deep.

Watch out

Kyler under pressure

Minnesota allows a sack on 9.9% of dropbacks (30th). Detroit's pass rush gets home on 7.6% (7th best). Drive killers are the main risk.

Wash

Ground game

Detroit is average against the run (19th in EPA), but Minnesota's run game is 28th. Expect volume for the backs, not efficiency.
