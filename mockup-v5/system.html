Spacing, type and logos

Every number on this page was measured on the live site (1.3.13) before it was proposed. Left or top is today; right or bottom is the fix. The logos are the real ESPN marks nflverse publishes, loaded the way the headshots are; flip the switch to see the drawn tiles, which is what the site shows when the setting is off.

1 · The scales

Today: fourteen text sizes, 123 runs of text at 8 or 9px, eleven letter-spacings on uppercase labels, panel insides padded six ways. Proposed: four spacing steps and seven type steps, nothing under 11px.

Spacing · four steps

s3 (14px) is the default edge inside every panel; s4 (22px) is the gap between bands. s1 and s2 are for inside small things: chips, cells, tile labels.

Type · seven steps

4 · The phone hero

At 375px today the headshot floats alone with about 160px of air around it, and the team link, snap badge, Share card, Compare and Players like him stack as five loose rows. Proposed: the headshot goes up beside the name as a 96px cutout, the team block becomes one row, the actions one wrapping row.
