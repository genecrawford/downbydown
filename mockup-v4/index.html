What this is

Down by Down is a free site built on public play-by-play data from nflverse. It updates every night during the season. EPA is expected points added. A play counts as a success when it gains 40% of the needed yards on 1st down, 60% on 2nd, and 100% on 3rd and 4th. Made by Period Three in Columbia, SC.
